# Privacy Policy - ProBrain
Effective date: September 25, 2026

ProBrain ("we," "our," or "the App") is a productivity application that helps users manage tasks, build habits, plan their days, track focus sessions, and maintain personal records.

This Privacy Policy explains how ProBrain collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ProBrain, we may process the following information:

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

Email address

Firebase authentication identifier (UID)

Authentication and account-related information

User-Generated Content

Information you enter into the app may include:

Tasks and planner entries

Habits and habit completion records

Diary entries and personal notes

Focus session records

Financial records and account details you choose to enter

App preferences and settings

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for using the app.

Guest Mode

ProBrain allows you to use certain features without creating an account. In guest mode, your information is stored locally on your device. If you choose to create an account, certain guest data may be transferred to your account, depending on the app's migration process.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Provide the app's features and functionality.

Save and synchronize your data across supported sessions and devices.

Maintain your account and authenticate your identity.

Support account deletion and data management.

Maintain the security and reliability of the app.

Troubleshoot technical issues and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services
Local Storage

ProBrain stores app data locally on your device using its local database. Guest-mode data is stored on your device and is not automatically associated with a cloud account.

Firebase

ProBrain uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication and cloud data storage.

Depending on your use of the app, information may be processed through:

Firebase Authentication: for account creation, sign-in, and authentication.

Cloud Firestore: for storing and synchronizing account-related app data.

Firebase services may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy 

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy 

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase to operate the app's features.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the app and its users.

5. Data Retention

We retain account-related information and user-generated content for as long as necessary to provide the app's services, unless deletion is requested or a longer retention period is required by law.

Guest-mode data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove it.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ProBrain account through the account deletion option available within the app.

When you delete your account:

Your account's cloud data is deleted from the app's Firestore data collections.

Your Firebase Authentication account is deleted.

Account-related local data is removed from the app's local database.

Guest-mode data is handled separately and is not intentionally deleted as part of deleting an authenticated account.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated data. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required by law.

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting:

Email: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]

Please include enough information for us to identify your account. Do not send your password.

7. Data Security

We use measures intended to protect your information, including account-based access controls and secure communication with supported services.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

ProBrain is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise manage your personal information.

You can manage your account and delete it through the app, or contact us using the email address provided below.
