# Account and Data Deletion

## How to Delete Your Account

ProBrain users can permanently delete their accounts directly from the app:

1. Open ProBrain and sign in.
2. Go to the account settings.
3. Select the account deletion option.
4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account and associated cloud data will be deleted.

## Request Deletion Without Access to the App

If you cannot access ProBrain, you can request account deletion using the form below.

**[Request Account Deletion](https://forms.gle/8nFH6aNS2qieRdh3A)**

Enter the email address associated with your ProBrain account and confirm your deletion request.

Do not share your password.

## What Data Is Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is deleted:

* Account information and authentication account.
* Tasks, habits, habit logs, diary entries, and planner data.
* Focus session records, preferences, and financial records associated with your account.

## What Data Is Retained?

Guest-mode data stored locally on your device is handled separately and is not intentionally deleted when you delete an authenticated account.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required by law.

## Deletion Timeline

Account deletion is processed through the app's account deletion feature. Requests submitted by email will be reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible.
